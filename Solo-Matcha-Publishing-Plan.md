## Workflow per article

Automation handles the brief, the first draft, formatting and scheduling. A person handles the facts, the first-hand detail and the final read.

1. Brief (automated): target question, the headings a good answer needs, and the sources to cite.
2. First draft (automated): written from the brief, with every number tied to a named source.
3. First-hand pass (you): add something only you have, such as a tasting note, a photo of the tin, or a price you checked yourself.
4. Fact check (you): open each cited page and confirm the number and the date.
5. Links and disclosure (automated): insert affiliate links and the disclosure line, and drop any health claim.
6. Publish and index (automated): schedule the post, then submit the URL in Search Console.
7. Refresh (automated reminder): re-check prices and availability 90 days after publishing.

## Publish checklist

An article goes live only when every box is ticked. Copy this list into each article's draft.

- [ ] Every number links to a page that was opened and checked
- [ ] Includes one first-hand detail: a photo, a tasting note or a price you checked
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure appears above the first affiliate link
- [ ] No claim that matcha treats, prevents or boosts anything
- [ ] Recommends only tins that list a single ingredient and a named origin
- [ ] Title, description and one internal link to a related guide are set
- [ ] Reads cleanly on a phone and in dark mode

## Monetization milestones

Apply to affiliate programs once five articles are live, then move to ad networks as traffic passes each threshold. Networks change their minimums often, so confirm each one on its own site before applying.

| Trigger | Step | Reported terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 articles live (about week 3) | Apply to Matcha Source and Mantra Matcha on ShareASale, DoMatcha through Lasso, and GoMatcha and Nippon Matcha on Awin | Matcha Source 9% per sale, Mantra Matcha 15%, DoMatcha up to 13.5%; Awin rates not confirmed |
| 1,000 monthly sessions | Apply to Journey by Mediavine | Lower RPM, about $5-15 per 1,000 sessions |
| 25,000 monthly pageviews | Apply to Raptive | One 2026 source reports this minimum since October 2025; older sources say 100,000 |
| 50,000 monthly sessions | Move to full Mediavine | Food and recipe RPM about $18-26 per 1,000 sessions in one comparison |

Planning target, not a forecast: 1,000 monthly sessions by the end of week 12. If the site is below 300 sessions by then, change the topic mix before writing more.

## Before the first article

The site design is a private preview, not yet live at solomatcha.com. These steps put it there.

- [ ] Remove the forwarding rule at your registrar that sends solomatcha.com to japan.go.jp
- [ ] Pick a publishing platform. A static site generator deployed from Git fits an automated pipeline, and WordPress is the alternative if you want a visual editor
- [ ] Point the domain's DNS at that host and confirm HTTPS works
- [ ] Add Google Search Console and a basic analytics tool so sessions are counted the way ad networks count them
- [ ] Add an email signup form to every article, since email is the one traffic source Google does not control
- [ ] Budget roughly $250-400 for the tins used in the week 6-7 tastings (my estimate)
- [ ] Write the affiliate disclosure page and link it from the footer
