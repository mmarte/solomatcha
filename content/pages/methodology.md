This page explains how the numbers and rankings on SoloMatcha are made, so you can check our work.

## Where the data comes from

Each product in the Data Center is entered by hand from the seller's own product page. We record the price, the size in grams, what the seller says about grade, region, cultivar and harvest year, and the date we checked. Every product page links to the page the price came from.

If a seller page does not state something, we write "Not stated". We do not fill gaps from guesswork or from other sites.

## How price per gram is calculated

Price per gram is the listed price divided by the grams in the tin. Price per cup uses 2 g of matcha per cup, the same amount as our whisking guide. Prices exclude shipping, tax and discounts. Prices change often, so treat any figure as correct on the date shown and check the seller before you buy.

We never convert between currencies. Tins priced in different currencies are ranked in separate lists, and a comparison between them shows both prices without a difference.

## Rankings

Our rankings currently order tins by price per gram, lowest first. A ranking page is only indexed by search engines once it ranks at least two tins. A price ranking says nothing about taste or quality.

## Quality scores

We do not publish a quality score yet. Before we do, we will publish the scoring criteria on this page and taste the tins ourselves. Until then, product pages show "Not scored yet", and our build refuses to publish a score.

## Comparisons

A comparison puts two tins side by side using the same fields as the Data Center. We choose which pairs to compare by what readers are likely to weigh against each other, not by who pays us.

## Affiliate links and independence

Some links to retailers are affiliate links. If you buy through one, SoloMatcha may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links never change a price, a ranking order or a comparison. Pages with affiliate links say so at the top. More detail is on our [disclosure page](/disclosure/).

## Health claims

We do not make health claims about matcha. Our build blocks guides that contain them.

## Corrections and old versions

If we find an error, we fix it and show the update date. When we rewrite a guide, the earlier version is kept in the [archive](/archive/) instead of being deleted.
