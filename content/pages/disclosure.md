SoloMatcha is a free website. It is paid for in two ways.

## Affiliate links

Some links to shops are affiliate links. If you buy after clicking one, the shop may pay SoloMatcha a commission. The price you pay does not change. Affiliate links are marked in the page with a small arrow, and pages that contain them say so near the top.

## Advertising

SoloMatcha may show ads from an advertising network. Advertisers do not see or approve what we write.

## What does not change

- We do not accept payment to recommend a product.
- We do not write about health benefits. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
- When we compare products, we say how we compared them.
- Corrections are welcome. Tell us what is wrong and we will fix it and note the change.
