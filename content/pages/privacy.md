SoloMatcha (solomatcha.com) is a content website. This page explains what information is collected when you visit and who collects it.

## What we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name or email address through this site. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps standard server logs and security data (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to deliver and protect the site.

## Fonts

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

## Analytics

SoloMatcha uses Google Tag Manager to load measurement tools, such as Google Analytics, which report how visitors use the site (pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser). These tools use cookies or similar identifiers and send data to Google. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Embedded video

Some pages offer a video from YouTube. A preview image loads from YouTube's image server when the page loads, which lets Google see your IP address. The video itself is not requested until you click play, and then it loads from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. YouTube (Google) may then set cookies or collect data under its own policy.

## Affiliate links

Some links on SoloMatcha go to retailers. If you click one, the retailer or its affiliate network may set a cookie so a purchase can be credited to us. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See [How SoloMatcha is paid](/disclosure/).

## Advertising

SoloMatcha plans to show ads served by third-party advertising networks. When ads are switched on, this page will name each network and link to its privacy policy and opt-out page. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and measure them, including ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Where the law requires it, you will be asked for your choices through a consent message before such cookies are set.

You can also opt out of personalised advertising through your browser settings, or through the industry opt-out pages at [optout.aboutads.info](https://optout.aboutads.info) and [youronlinechoices.com](https://www.youronlinechoices.com).

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold an account for you, most requests are best directed to the third parties above. You can still contact us about anything on this page.

## Children

SoloMatcha is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
