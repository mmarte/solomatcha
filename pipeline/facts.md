## Prices and supply
- Kyoto wholesale tencha prices rose by roughly 170% from 2024 to 2025 (Tezumi, reported by Glenbrook Torch): https://torch.glenbrook225.org/news/2026/02/28/matcha-mania-drives-tea-shortage/
- Ippodo, a Kyoto tea company, posted a notice dated 2025-08-01 about higher tencha costs (about 2.7 times the May 2025 Kyoto rate) and purchase limits: https://global.ippodo-tea.co.jp/blogs/news/20250801
- A Japanese news report put the April 2026 average tencha price at 8,235 yen per kg, about 1.7 times the prior year. Primary source not yet located.

## Demand and trade
- 78% of Japan's powdered green tea exports went to the US in 2024: https://www.local10.com/news/local/2025/09/04/american-matcha-craze-has-made-popular-tea-difficult-to-keep-on-shelves/
- Matcha was about half of the 8,798 tonnes of green tea Japan exported in 2024, and 7 to 8% of Japan's tea production: https://www.nerdwallet.com/finance/learn/matcha-price
- US retail matcha sales grew 86% over three years, per NIQ: https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/09/16/business/matcha-price-increases/

## Tariffs
- One vendor (O-cha) states the 15% US tariff on tea under HTS 0902.10 was removed on 2025-11-13. Single vendor source; verify before citing: https://www.o-cha.com/news/tariff-reduction-on-green-tea-and-matcha-products

## Grading
- A vendor quoted in the press says Japan has no formal matcha grading guideline: https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/753780

## Preparation (house standard used on the home page)
- Usucha: 2 g matcha, 70 ml water, about 80 °C, whisk about 15 seconds in a zigzag motion.
